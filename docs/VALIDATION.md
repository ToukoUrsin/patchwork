### Bugs found live and fixed

1. **Every workflow write rewrote the public edition.** `planTransaction` patched every document (with `ifRevisionID`) even when unchanged. In Sanity a guarded patch always writes a new `_rev`, so the English edit in step 5 moved the public edition from `_rev LCntMInbokDNzSR9NHNiQL` to `lK8ThWNLjNn912fmFZv2zm` without a content change, and every command re-sent all revision receipts. Verified with a throwaway draft document (created and deleted): an empty guarded patch and an identical `set` both bump `_rev`; a stale empty patch is rejected with 409. Fix: decision inputs (manifest, entries, sources) are still guarded on every write; unchanged `patchworkPublication` and `patchworkRevision` documents are no longer touched. After the fix, 7 further commands left the lantern edition at `lK8ThWNLjNn912fmFZv2zm`, and the loom edition kept `_rev LCntMInbokDNzSR9NHVGZ5` through later loom and garden writes. Covered by the updated transaction test.
2. **A still-correct translation could not be re-based.** After an English edit, saving an unchanged Finnish/Spanish draft is how lineage is repaired, but the save button required a form change. The button now reads “Confirm against EN vN” for a stale, unchanged translation. Used live in step 6 and in the film.
